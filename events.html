Main Events of the Year

Join us for our flagship events throughout the academic year

Qiskit Fall Fest

Fall Semester

Join us for the Qiskit Fall Fest, a celebration of quantum computing featuring workshops, talks, and hands-on sessions with Qiskit. This event brings together students, researchers, and industry professionals to explore the latest developments in quantum computing.

Career Fair

Spring Semester

Connect with leading companies and research institutions in the quantum computing field. Our Career Fair provides opportunities to network, learn about internships and job openings, and discover career paths in quantum technologies.

Seminars

Learn from experts in quantum computing research and industry

Our seminar series features cutting-edge research and trends from leaders in industry and academia. These sessions provide insights into the latest developments in quantum computing, quantum algorithms, quantum hardware, and applications across various fields.

Stay tuned for upcoming seminar announcements! We regularly host talks from researchers, industry professionals, and graduate students working at the forefront of quantum technologies.

Mentorship Program

Get personalized guidance from experienced mentors

Our mentorship program pairs undergraduate students with graduate student mentors who provide skill-focused support in quantum computing. Whether you're just starting your quantum journey or looking to deepen your understanding, our mentors are here to help.
