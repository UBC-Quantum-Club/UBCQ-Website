ABOUT UBCQ

Canada's second oldest undergraduate quantum club, the University of British Columbia Quantum Club (UBCQ) is dedicated to promoting awareness and provide assistance to undergraduate students in the field of quantum. We host seminars, workshops, career fairs, and more to this end.

OUR 4
PILLARS

Community

Foster lasting connections among students interested in quantum technologies.

Education

Engage future scholars with hands-on projects and peer-facilitated learning.

Equity

Advocate for accessibility and representation within the field.

Career

Connect students to leading research and professional opportunities.
